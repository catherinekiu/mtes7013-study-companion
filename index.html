Suggested argument flow

  1. Define the trend, issue or challenge.
  2. Establish the Malaysian primary mathematics context.
  3. Present credible evidence.
  4. Explain its impact on curriculum implementation or learning.
  5. Connect it with current educational practice.
  6. Introduce an international perspective.
  7. Evaluate strengths, tensions or limitations.
  8. State your group's position.
  9. Justify one recommendation.
  10. Conclude without overstating the evidence.

Evidence & Sources

Use evidence responsibly. This prototype does not invent or verify external references.

Academic integrity
Never invent: DOI, authors, journals, statistics, curriculum policies or international results. If evidence is missing, say so.

Preferred evidence hierarchy

  1. Official curriculum documents and ministry documents.
  2. OECD / IEA / international assessment documentation.
  3. Peer-reviewed research.
  4. Recognised mathematics education organisations.
